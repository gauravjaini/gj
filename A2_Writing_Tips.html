Mission: Pass on Monday

You have limited grammar and 2 hours a day. Your strategy is Simplicity. Do not try to be creative. Do not use complex sentences. Follow the rules below to survive.

1

The Golden Rule: SVOMP

Dutch word order is strict. Always follow this order to be safe.

Subject
Verb
Object
Time/Place
Ik
heb
een afspraak
morgen.
Hij
drinkt
water
in de keuken.
2

The "Want" Trick

NEVER use "Omdat". It changes the word order. Use "Want" (because). It acts like a period.

Don't do this (Hard):

Ik kom niet, omdat ik ziek ben.

DO THIS (Easy):

Ik kom niet, want ik ben ziek.

(Sentence 1 + want + Sentence 2)

Exam Pattern Analysis (2022-2026)

Based on past exams, this is what you are most likely to face. We focus our 2 hours/day on the biggest chunks.

The 4-Day Sprint Schedule

2 Hours per day. Strict focus. No distractions.

  • Hour 1: Master "Ik / Jij / Hij / Wij". Write 20 sentences about yourself. (e.g., "Ik werk...", "Ik woon...", "Ik heb...").
  • Hour 2: Practice "WANT". Connect two simple sentences using 'want'. (e.g., "Ik ga slapen. Ik ben moe." -> "Ik ga slapen, want ik ben moe.")
  • Homework: Memorize the "Safe Opener" for emails: "Ik schrijf deze e-mail, want..."
  • Hour 1: The Structure. Greeting ("Beste [Naam],"), The Reason ("Ik schrijf want..."), The Action ("Ik kom niet."), Closing ("Met vriendelijke groet,").
  • Hour 2: Problem Solving. Practice writing emails for: Calling in sick, Rescheduling an appointment, Inviting a friend.
  • Tip: Use the "Solver" tab today to copy the structure of the sample emails.
  • Hour 1: Forms. Learn words like "Geslacht" (Sex), "Geboortedatum" (DOB), "Reden" (Reason). Practice inventing fake data quickly.
  • Hour 2: The "Wijkkrant" (Short Text). Describe a picture in 3 sentences. Subject + Verb + Object. Don't interpret thoughts, describe actions.
  • Drill: Look at 5 random photos on your phone and write 2 sentences for each in Dutch.
  • Hour 1: Memory Bank. Go to the "Memory Bank" tab and quiz yourself until you can say the Dutch sentence instantly after reading the English.
  • Hour 2: Mock Exam. Pick 4 random prompts from the "Solver" tab. Set a timer for 30 minutes. Write the answers without help. Check your punctuation.
  • Night Before: Sleep well. Don't study after 8 PM.

Model Answers for YOUR PDF Exercises

These answers use the "Safe Strategy": Short sentences, SVOMP order, and "Want". Memorize the structure, not just the words.

Exercise: Afspraak verzetten (Reschedule Appointment)

Prompt: Cancel appointment with Amber, say why (invent reason), propose new date.

Your Task Requirements

  • Write to Amber.
  • Say you want to reschedule.
  • Reason: Working? Sick?
  • New date suggestion.

Model Answer (Safe)

Beste Amber,


Ik wil onze afspraak verzetten.

Ik kan morgen niet komen, want ik moet werken.

Kunnen wij op woensdag afspreken?


Met vriendelijke groet,

[Jouw Naam]

From PDF 1

Exercise: Feest (Short Text)

Prompt: Write about a party you celebrate. Why? Who comes? What do you do?

Model Answer (Safe)

Ik vier elk jaar mijn verjaardag.

Mijn familie en vrienden komen op het feest.

Wij eten taart en wij luisteren naar muziek.

Ik vind het feest heel leuk.

Note: Keep it generic. "Birthday" is the easiest party to describe because the vocabulary (taart, muziek, familie) is simple.
